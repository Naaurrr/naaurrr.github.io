NeuroArm - Wireless EEG-Controlled Robotic Arm

Sep 2024 – May 2025 • CSUB Senior Design Expo 2025 • Demonstrated live

Led a 3-member team building a 2-DOF wireless robotic arm proof of concept controlled by EEG brain signals. Owned the full embedded firmware and wireless communication layer from concept to live demonstration at the Senior Design Expo.

The original platform was an STM32 for EEG processing and Arduino for servo communication. When a teammate could not generate compatible firmware for the STM32 within our timeline, and the university firewall blocked Arduino's IoT cloud entirely, I proposed pivoting the full communication layer to an ESP32 in SoftAP mode. This created a direct Wi-Fi link to MATLAB without needing the university network, resolved both blockers simultaneously, and let me write the full C++ firmware myself: UDP command parsing, PWM servo control for three motors, and ACK-based handshake logic for reliable delivery. Delivered a working live demo at the Senior Design Expo within two weeks of the pivot.

MPU-6050 Flex PCB Breakout

May 2026 – Present • KiCad 7 • Fabricated at JLCPCB

Designed a single-layer flexible PCB breakout for the MPU-6050 6-axis IMU in KiCad. Polyimide substrate, ENIG finish, 0603 and 0201 SMD components. Custom schematic symbol for the MPU-6050 GY-521 module. Laser-cut star profile with silkscreen artwork. Board dimensions: 37.21 × 38.07mm. C1 (100nF decoupling) placed within 2.5mm of VCC pin. LED resistor calculated at 130Ω.

Assembled using syringe solder paste and tape fixturing during reflow. Post-assembly DMM continuity testing isolated a reversed LED polarity fault in the footprint orientation. Root cause documented and corrected in the Rev B schematic. This project is ongoing and actively in progress.

Servo-PID-Tuning - Hardware System Identification

Mar 2026 • MATLAB / Arduino

Performed hardware-in-the-loop system identification on hobby servo motors. Arduino swept 1000–2000 μs PWM while reading potentiometer angle feedback via a 3D-printed mechanical coupling at 50 Hz. MATLAB: tfest → 2nd-order TF at 88.8% fit. ZOH discretization at Ts = 0.021 s. pidtune → Kp 14.50, Ki 98.12, Kd 0.14. Closed-loop simulation validated. Gains not deployed to hardware.
